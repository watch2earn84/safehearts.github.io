Privacy Policy

Pamir Cooperative Utility Society (PCUS) is committed to protecting consumer privacy. All personal and account details collected during billing or mobile registration are handled securely on-device and strictly used for utility management.

Terms & Conditions

By accessing PCUS digital services or registering for electricity connections, consumers agree to comply with local utility billing schedules, grid safety guidelines, and payment terms.

Return / Refund Policy

Digital utility payments and tariff billings processed through our portal are subject to account verification. Excess or erroneous charges will be adjusted against subsequent billing cycles or refunded to the original payment method within 7–10 business days upon written request.

Shipping / Service Delivery Policy

Utility services, digital bill receipts, and meter activation tokens are delivered instantly via digital channels or mobile notifications. Physical hardware items (such as energy meters) are delivered and installed locally by PCUS field technicians within 3–5 business days after application processing.
