Frequently Asked Questions

Is WhatsClean safe to use?

Yes. WhatsClean performs operations entirely on-device. It requires no network permissions, guaranteeing that your personal media and file paths remain strictly confidential and private.

How does cleaning by date protect my data?

Unlike regular file clearers that wipe whole directories, WhatsClean allows you to specify a timeframe rule. For instance, you can delete clutter older than three months while preserving your recent business transactions and images safely.

Can it find specific files lost in my chat folders?

Yes. WhatsClean scans and tracks structural file types across hidden chat database locations, instantly lifting buried assignment PDFs, invoices, and documents so you can save or share them without scrolling history strings.

Can I back up status videos privately?

Yes. You can view, play, and download temporary video updates directly inside the app wrapper without letting the uploader know you have opened their status card.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. The app contains built-in cross-directory compatibility to let you manage your personal media storage folder and business data modules independently.
